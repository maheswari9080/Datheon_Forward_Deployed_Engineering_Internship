# Master Services Agreement (MSA) - Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure

Date: 2026-03-15
Department: legal
Clearance: Tier 2

## 1. Scope of Services
Vendor shall provide high-availability managed vector database clusters, automated ETL pipelines, and API gateways for enterprise operations. Service Level Agreement guarantees 99.99% monthly uptime.

## 2. Data Protection and Tenant Isolation
Vendor strictly commits to cryptographic tenant isolation. Customer data from Tenant Alpha shall never intermingle with Tenant Beta under any query execution plan. 

## 3. Incident Notification and Breach Penalties
Any security incident or unauthorized access attempt must be reported to the Customer Security Operations Center within two hours of confirmation. Liquidated damages for unnotified downtime shall accrue at $10,000 per hour.
